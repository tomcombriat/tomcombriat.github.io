# Klikken XVI

I got to play at Klikken, a small event gathering people interested in synthesizers and/or noise/experimental music to share their music and perform live.
At Klikken you find quite a variety of way to produce sound and experimental music… Big modular synthesizers, contact microphones, feedbacking mixing tables and custom electronics are common! More standard instruments can also be seen, here and there, but they are usually *not* played in a standard way…

With 10 (11?) sets that night, it was possible to listen to a wide range of experimental music and it was a pleasure to play in that context! That was the occasion to play [Bølgene](/projects/boelgene) in a live context for the first time. Amazing that the rushed soldering work of this prototype survived…

Thanks to Øyvind for the invitation and organization!
